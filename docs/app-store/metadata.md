## English

- Name: MicFirst
- Subtitle: Your microphones, in order
- Category: Utilities
- Price: Free
- Description:

MicFirst is a small macOS menu bar utility for managing the system
default audio input device.

It gives microphones and other input devices a native-feeling Sound menu of
their own. Pick an input device from the menu, adjust input volume when the
device supports it, and arrange microphones in priority order. MicFirst chooses
the first available device, falls back when it disconnects, and restores the
preferred route after external changes.

MicFirst works locally on your Mac and does not collect data.

MicFirst 是一个小型 macOS 菜单栏工具，用于管理系统默认音频输入设备。
